# Plan: Send 15 job applications

**Deadline:** Tuesday, 15 December 2026, 17:00 WIB (UTC+7)
**Planned:** Monday, 23 November 2026 (22 days out), with Gemini

## How the plan was made
I talked the deadline through with Gemini: the state of my resume, portfolio and cover
letter, how much tailoring each application gets (45 to 60 minutes), how many roles I had
saved (none), and how much time I have on weekdays versus weekends. Gemini worked backward
from the deadline into six milestones. Prep comes first so the roughly 15-hour application
sprint isn't slowed by missing materials, and the batches land on weekends, when I have the
most time.

## Milestones

| # | Date | T-minus | Action | Done when |
|---|------|---------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Sun 29 Nov | T-16 | Finish CV, GitHub, cover letter | Resume is exported to a clean PDF, GitHub READMEs (including the countdown project) are polished, and the base cover letter template is ready |
| 2 | Thu 3 Dec | T-12 | Shortlist 25 target roles | 25 active, high-fit roles are saved in a tracker with links, deadlines and priority rankings |
| 3 | Sun 6 Dec | T-9 | Submit first 4 applications | The first 4 tailored applications are sent, proving the 45 to 60 minute tailoring workflow holds up |
| 4 | Thu 10 Dec | T-5 | Reach 8 of 15 apps sent | 8 applications are submitted across the week, the halfway mark, with no lingering drafts |
| 5 | Sun 13 Dec | T-2 | Reach 12 of 15 apps sent | 12 applications are submitted after a 4-application weekend push, leaving 3 for Mon and Tue |
| 6 | Tue 15 Dec, 17:00 | T-0 | Submit final 3 apps (15/15) | All 15 applications are submitted, confirmation emails are archived, and the tracker is fully logged |

Batches: 4 + 4 + 4 + 3 = 15.

## Why 25 roles for 15 applications
Some roles will close or turn out to be a poor fit once I read the full posting. A 10-role
buffer means a dropped role never stalls a batch.

## Not in this plan
No dedicated follow-up milestone. Follow-ups on the first applications can fit into the
Dec 10 to Dec 13 window if replies are slow.

## Daily habit
Each morning at 08:00 WIB a calendar reminder shows the days left and the next milestone
(see `reminders.ics`). After each application, bump `progress.current` in `config.yaml` and
re-render the lock screen so the progress bar stays honest.

The lock screen shows shortened "done when" checks so they fit on a phone; the full checks
are above.
