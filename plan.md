# Plan: Send 15 job applications

**Deadline:** Tuesday, 15 December 2026, 17:00 WIB (UTC+7)
**Start:** Sunday, 22 November 2026 (23 days out)

Working backward from the deadline: the last 5 applications land on the deadline day, so the
first 10 must be out a week earlier, which means the target list and resume must be ready in
the first week. Follow-ups sit between batches so replies can arrive before the deadline.

## Why 25 roles for 15 applications
Some roles will close or turn out to be a poor fit once you read the full posting. A 10-role
buffer means a dropped role never stalls a batch.

## Daily habit
Each morning at 08:00 WIB a calendar reminder shows the days left and the next milestone
(see `reminders.ics`). After each application, bump `progress.current` in `config.yaml` and
re-render the lock screen so the progress bar stays honest.
